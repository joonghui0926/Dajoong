Pause acquisition immediately if any of the following occurs:

- a submission enters the conversion queue without accepted terms;
- a drawing is used publicly without a separate publication opt-in;
- a model is described as construction-ready before qualified review;
- the exact model hashes do not match the qualification manifest;
- the campaign attracts unqualified consumer rendering requests instead of AEC workflows;
- support capacity exceeds five concurrent accepted pilots.
