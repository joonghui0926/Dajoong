# Dajoong message house

## Category

**Evidence-linked drawing-to-BIM compiler**

Dajoong is not a replacement for Autodesk or Procore. Those products are most
valuable after a usable model exists. Dajoong addresses the missing first mile:
turning a drawing archive into reviewable, editable openBIM artifacts.

## Master line

> From drawing to editable BIM — with the evidence still attached.

Short form:

> The missing first mile of BIM.

## Who it is for

### Primary wedge

Organizations responsible for existing buildings, renovations, tenant
improvements, and recurring field work where the authoritative information is
still trapped in PDFs or raster floor plans.

### Buyers

- BIM and VDC leads who need a usable starting model without another manual redraw
- Facility and asset managers who need structured information from legacy plans
- Architecture and engineering practices that repeatedly rebuild existing conditions
- BIM consultants who can deliver more projects when first-pass modeling is accelerated

### Not the first target

- Consumers seeking interior-design renders
- Ground-up projects with current, coordinated authoring models
- Buyers demanding an unreviewed construction model from an unknown raster

## Three proof pillars

### 1. Small inference, explicit geometry

The bundled primary checkpoint has 86,533 parameters and runs through ONNX on
CPU. It proposes evidence. A deterministic compiler resolves metric geometry,
topology, and export rules.

### 2. The source survives the conversion

Stable element IDs, page references, file hashes, confidence, and review state
remain connected to the output. The result is inspectable rather than a visual
black box.

### 3. Open, editable handoff

IFC is the canonical authoring exchange. A colored semantic GLB supports fast
web and mobile review. Each wall, opening, room, and supported fixed object is a
selectable node.

## Competitive framing

### Versus scan-to-CAD services

Do not claim that drawing conversion replaces field verification. Say:

> Twindo starts with a new spatial scan and sells professional CAD delivery by
> area. Dajoong starts with the drawings an organization already owns and turns
> them into an evidence-linked model that remains visibly review-gated.

### Versus Plans2BIM

Plans2BIM validates the market for browser-based plan-to-IFC conversion and
manual correction. Dajoong should differentiate on a compact private CPU
runtime, source-linked entities, explicit qualification, fail-closed export,
and one editor shared across web and mobile. Do not claim superiority without a
head-to-head benchmark.

### Versus Autodesk and Procore

> Autodesk and Procore help teams coordinate and manage models. Dajoong creates
> the reviewable starting model when the project still begins with drawings.

The strategic goal is integration and marketplace distribution, not a broad
feature-by-feature attack.

## Objection handling

### “We already have BIM.”

Use Dajoong for the buildings, floors, and revisions that do not. The wedge is
portfolio inconsistency, not replacing a healthy authoring workflow.

### “We can outsource manual modeling cheaply.”

Compare more than the model file. Ask whether the service preserves source
references, review state, stable IDs, repeatable revisions, and a machine-readable
verification record. Dajoong competes on repeatability and auditability before price.

### “Can we build from it?”

No unqualified promise. Dajoong output is review-gated. A qualified project
professional must approve dimensions, assemblies, and coordination before construction use.

### “Will our drawings train your model?”

Not by default. Conversion content is not used for training without separate written permission.

### “Why not just use Autodesk?”

Autodesk is a destination and integration surface. Dajoong reduces the manual
work required to create the initial openBIM asset from legacy drawings.
