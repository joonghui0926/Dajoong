# Competitive and market evidence — August 9, 2026

This document records why the launch strategy focuses on missing-model creation,
evidence, and review rather than generic collaboration or photorealistic output.

## Market facts

### Existing information is a recognized openBIM problem

buildingSMART's Retro BIM use case calls for geometrically verified models of
existing assets with structured, standardized export. It accepts available
project information as input and identifies IFC as a way to reduce information
loss. This directly supports an existing-building and drawing-archive wedge.

Source:
https://ucm-old.buildingsmart.org/use-case-details/1646/en

### IFC carries more than visible geometry

buildingSMART describes IFC as a vendor-neutral standard that captures identity,
semantics, attributes, and relationships. Dajoong should therefore market
source-linked semantic handoff rather than only a 3D image.

Source:
https://technical.buildingsmart.org/standards/ifc/

### Autodesk is creating a discovery surface for minimal-BIM tools

Autodesk's 2026 Design and Make Marketplace page explicitly frames the problem:
many buildings do not have a full BIM model and their documentation remains in
CAD, PDFs, scans, and hand-drawn plans. The publishing process is estimated at
one hour for onboarding, two to three hours for listing preparation, and one to
three weeks for review. This is an important post-pilot distribution path.

Source:
https://aps.autodesk.com/publish

### buildingSMART awards reward open and evidenced workflows

The 2026 openBIM Awards are open to projects demonstrating innovative use of
open standards. Submission is free. A 2025 winner included a scan-to-BIM dataset
and benchmark. A permissioned field-test corpus and Build Sheet can later support
a credible technical entry.

Source:
https://www.buildingsmart.org/openbim-awards-program/

## Competitor facts

### Twindo

Twindo's official June 2026 pricing describes a scan-first service priced by
area. Starter pricing lists 3D SketchUp/Revit/Chief Architect/Vectorworks at
$0.26/sqft and 2D floor plans at $0.18/sqft, with lower Essentials prices.
Twindo supports several professional CAD formats and includes human-delivery
service economics. Dajoong must not compare raw inference cost to this full
service price without matching capture, modeling, review, and format scope.

Source:
https://support.twindo.com/article/716-how-does-scan-to-cad-pricing-work

### Plans2BIM / WiseBIM

The buildingSMART case entry says Plans2BIM uploads PDF/PNG/JPEG plans, asks for
scale and height, recognizes structural elements, provides a 2D/3D viewer and
editor, exports IFC and CSV/XLSX, and assembles floors. Its listed buyers include
engineering offices, facility/property managers, construction companies, and
architects. This confirms both market demand and the need to differentiate on
evidence, qualification, privacy, compact runtime, and reviewer workflow.

Source:
https://awards.buildingsmart.org/gallery/PzBxRjWa/GeJNaAxB

### Autodesk and Procore

Autodesk and Procore emphasize collaboration, coordination, issue management,
mobile model access, and downstream project workflows. They are complements and
distribution/integration targets for Dajoong's first-mile conversion, not the
right objects for a broad replacement narrative.

Sources:
https://construction.autodesk.com/
https://www.procore.com/bim

## Strategic conclusion

The defensible wedge is:

> Existing drawing in; source-linked, review-gated openBIM out.

The campaign should prove this on difficult, permissioned drawings and publish
the review burden along with successful geometry. This creates a stronger trust
signal than a highlight reel and produces the design-partner evidence needed for
future marketplace, award, integration, and pricing decisions.
