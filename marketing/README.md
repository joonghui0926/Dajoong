# Dajoong marketing launch room

This directory is the approval-ready launch kit for Dajoong. Nothing in it
authorizes external publication, outreach, paid media, or customer data use.

## Recommended launch

Launch **The 100 Drawings Without BIM Project** under the positioning:

> The missing first mile of BIM.

Dajoong will invite owners, architects, BIM consultants, and facility teams to
nominate one legacy floor-plan sheet. Each selected sheet receives a private
conversion and a **BIM Build Sheet** that records what the compiler created,
what remains review-required, and which IFC/GLB artifacts were produced.
Nothing becomes public without written opt-in.

The campaign is deliberately different from a generic free trial:

1. It turns product evidence into the content.
2. It builds a permissioned benchmark and design-partner pipeline.
3. It rewards difficult, commercially relevant drawings instead of cherry-picked demos.
4. It makes review gates a trust feature rather than hiding uncertainty.
5. Every accepted submission creates a natural follow-up with the team that owns the drawing.

## Launch sequence

1. Approve the no-spend pilot in `approval/APPROVAL_CARD.md`.
2. Publish the campaign landing copy in `campaigns/100_DRAWINGS.md`.
3. Open 20 private nomination slots and recruit five BIM consultants as referrers.
4. Run each accepted sheet through the sealed production model pair.
5. Deliver the private model, Build Sheet, and a 20-minute review session.
6. Request separate consent for a redacted public case card.
7. Publish honest weekly results and invite the next nominations.

## Marketing rule

Dajoong sells **traceable acceleration**, not unqualified automation. Public
materials must never claim arbitrary-drawing 100% accuracy, construction-ready
output without review, a universal conversion time, or a universal cost per
square foot unless an exact production model and sealed benchmark support it.
